About HomeBound

Solving the move-out problem at FHSU

Every semester, good belongings get left behind in Hays while new students pay full price for the same things. We think there's a better way.

The Problem Our Solution
The Problem

Thousands of students move out. Their stuff often doesn't make it.

At the end of every semester, students across Fort Hays State pack up their dorms and apartments. Desks, mini-fridges, lamps, microwaves, and bookshelves that were bought just months earlier are suddenly too big to ship, too awkward to carry, and too much trouble to sell in the last week of finals.

Graduating and international students feel this the most. Many are leaving Hays for good — sometimes leaving the country — and simply can't take everything with them. Without a car, meeting a buyer across town isn't realistic, and renting a storage unit costs $40–$115 a month.

A few weeks later, the next wave of students arrives needing the exact same items — and most of them end up paying full retail price.

✈️

Students can't take it with them

Graduating and international students leave Hays with only what fits in a suitcase or car.

🚗

No car, no sale

Many students have no way to meet buyers off campus or haul furniture across town.

💸

New students overpay

Incoming students buy brand-new items that a classmate just left behind.

🗑️

Usable items get thrown away

When there's no easy option, perfectly good belongings end up in the dumpster.

Our Solution

HomeBound keeps belongings moving — from one student to the next.

HomeBound is a web and mobile marketplace built only for the FHSU community. Students and faculty can list, search, buy, and sell items with people they share a campus with. And for students who are leaving before their item sells, HomeBound offers temporary on-campus storage: drop it off, and we hold it, list it, and pay you when it sells.

For sellers

Turn move-out into money

Instead of leaving things behind, list them in under a minute — or leave them with us and head home.

  • Free to list, 10% fee only when it sells
  • Campus drop-off — no car needed
  • Track your item from drop-off to sold
  • Get paid automatically
