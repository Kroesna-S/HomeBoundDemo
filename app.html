Help

How does storage work?

Pick a drop-off time when you list. Bring the item to the campus storage point. We photograph it, list it, and hold it until it sells. The buyer picks it up at a set time.

What does it cost?

Browsing and listing are free. HomeBound takes a 10% fee on completed sales, which covers running the platform and storage.

What if my item doesn't sell?

We notify you and suggest a lower price. After the storage limit, you pick it up or it is donated.

Who can sign in?

Anyone with an @mail.fhsu.edu or @fhsu.edu email. We send a 6-digit code to your inbox. HomeBound never sees or stores your university password.
